Methodology

Pipeline: raw deliveries to ranked players

Every state variable is computed from deliveries strictly before the ball being scored. Every model that touches outcomes (win probability, expected runs, calibration) is fitted leave-one-season-out, so a match's own result never informs its own context.

Step 2 · Defining pressure

Leverage index vs the usual shortcuts

Leverage = expected |ΔWP| over the empirical outcome mix of that phase, normalised so an average ball = 1 (after Tango's baseball Leverage Index). A ball is "pressure" when it is in the top quartile. Competing definitions are kept and compared below.

Chase leverage by over × wickets down

Mean LI, second innings. Darker = higher stakes.

Definition head-to-head

"Decided" = WP <10% or >90% (game effectively over). A good pressure definition avoids those balls and captures outsized win-probability movement.
Step 3 · Models

Six ideas of "delivering under pressure"

Each is empirical-Bayes shrunk with match-clustered standard errors (the match, not the ball, is the independent unit). Near-duplicate models are grouped into families and each family gets one vote.
